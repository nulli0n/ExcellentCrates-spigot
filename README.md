**ExcellentCrates** it is a modern, feature-rich, modular crate & lootbox plugin with an intuitive in-game GUI editor and smooth animations. Easily set up custom rewards, batch openings, portable crates, and fine-tuned limits without touching config files.

# 🔵 Features

- **In-Game GUI Editor** - Build your crates, keys, and rewards right in-game with easy menus, single-click buttons, and clean text popups.
- **Modularity & Extensibility** - Turn off anything you don't need so it never bugs you, or easily plug in your own custom animations and features with the dev API.
- **Infinite Keys** - Make as many keys as you want, link multiple keys to one crate, or turn any item in your hand into a key with a couple of clicks.
- **Custom Rewards & Hype Broadcasts** - Hand out unlimited rewards with items and commands, set limits and cooldowns, and shout out big wins to the whole server in chat!
- **Pickable Rewards** - Create chill, non-gambling crates where players simply pick the exact reward they want!
- **Cool Opening Animations** - Treat players to fun opening rollouts, customize pre-made animation profiles, or code your own from scratch.
- **Blocks, Particles & Holograms** - Drop crates anywhere in one click, style them with vanilla particles, and show built-in floating holograms with zero hassle.
- **Bulk Opening** - Got tons of keys? Skip the slow grind and pop open as many crates as you want all at once through a neat little menu.
- **Instant Fast Opening** - Don't feel like waiting on animations or menus? Just hold Shift-click and grab your loot on the spot.
- **Easy Key & Payment Choice** - Let players pick how they want to pay or which key to use, while the menu shows what they can afford.
- **Portable Crates** - Turn crates into pocket-sized lootboxes that players can carry around in their inventory or pick up off the ground.
- **Reward Previews** - Let players see what's inside before opening, showing off cool icons, chances, limits, and lore with placeholders.
- **Opening History** - Keep an eye on who opened what, which keys were used, and what dropped via clean console logs and log files.
